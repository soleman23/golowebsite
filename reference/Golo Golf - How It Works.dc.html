HANDICAPS, WITHOUT THE ARGUMENT

Everyone plays off the low man.

HOW THE MATH LANDS

Two games, four players, two payments.

Same foursome. Team Nassau — Mike & Tom against Sarah & Jess — at $5 a man with one press on the back, plus $2 skins with carryovers. Here's what GoLo shows on the 18th green.

Nassau: lost the front (−$5), won the back, the press and the 18 (+$15) = +$10 for Mike & Tom. Skins: each skin pays $2 from each of the other three — so a skin is worth $6, and every skin someone else wins costs you $2.

WHAT GOLO DOESN'T DO

A scorekeeper. Not a bookie.

✕
Hold or move money

GoLo tracks who owes who. You pay each other however your group already does. We never see a payment.

✕
Play the house

No odds, no lines, no rake. Just friendly wagers between the people in your group, at stakes you agreed on.

✕
Make everyone download it

One phone scores the round. Live sync so all four can enter their own scores is on the list, not built yet.

✕
Require an official handicap

No GHIN needed. Use a real index, the number your group settled on years ago, or play straight up.
