Every press logged the second it's called.

HANDICAPS & STROKES IN THE APP TODAY

The 6 and the 22 can play for real money.

Index in, course handicap out, strokes allocated by the card's stroke index — including the second stroke on the hardest holes when somebody needs 24 of them. Play it full, play it at 90%, or play straight up; the bets follow whatever you chose.

✓No GHIN required — type an index, or let the group agree on a number.
✓Slope-aware — the same index gives different strokes at different tees.
✓Allowance settings — match play off the low ball, or full handicap.
AUTO SETTLE-UP IN THE APP TODAY

Four players, six debts, three payments.

Nassau, presses, skins, the purse and every greenie get netted into one number per player, then reduced to the fewest transfers that make everyone whole. You read the list out loud in the parking lot and it's over — no daisy chain, no arithmetic with a beer in your hand.

✓Who pays whom — names and amounts, in plain order.
✓Mark paid — tick it off as the cash or the transfer lands.
✓Itemized — tap any number to see which bet produced it.
