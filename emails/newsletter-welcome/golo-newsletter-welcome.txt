DID YOU KNOW
You can run several games in the same round. Play Skins, a Nassau and Closest to the Pin together, and GoLo tracks each one and nets it all into one settle-up.

GAMES GOLO RUNS
Skins, Nassau, Scramble, Closest to the Pin, Longest Drive, Team Play, plus Wolf and Bingo Bango Bongo. Pick the format, set the amount, and GoLo keeps the tally.

WHAT TO EXPECT
Feature updates, how-tos and news. New features as they ship, quick how-tos for getting more out of the app, game rules and the math behind them, and news from the GoLo team.

WHAT GOLO DOES
- Track it: one live scorecard for the group.
- Score it: run several side games at once.
- Settle it: see exactly who owes whom.
